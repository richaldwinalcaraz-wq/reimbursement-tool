5-Step Process
1
Report Verification & Approval
VA reviews all reimbursement details in Billing Sheet. Once verified as accurate, clicks "YES" toggle to trigger automation.
2
Automated Data Retrieval
System automatically retrieves billing information, identifies recipients (Primary, CC, BCC), and fetches corresponding PDF reports from SharePoint.
3
Dynamic Email Generation
System analyzes two conditions (Recoveries & Past Due Balance) and selects appropriate email template (A, B, C, or D) with customized messaging.
4
Email Delivery via HubSpot
Email automatically sent to Primary Contact with PDF attachment. CC and BCC recipients included as configured. System captures HubSpot ticket number.
5
Automated Tracking & Audit Trail
Ticket number automatically written back to Billing Sheet. Complete activity log maintained including timestamp, ticket ID, and delivery status.
